Your stage. Your pace. Your moment.

LiveMuse is a fake live streaming simulator for creativity, confidence, and a little fun. Step into a private practice room with a virtual audience, friendly comments, floating energy, and surprise gifts. Rehearse your next introduction, tell a story, or simply get comfortable being on camera.

PRACTICE WITHOUT THE PRESSURE
Start a simulated live session whenever inspiration strikes. No account, followers, or internet connection required. Everything happens on your device.

MAKE THE ROOM YOURS
Choose your creator name and room title. Pick a Cozy, Buzzing, or Spotlight audience and set the mood with Aurora, Sunset, or Midnight studio backgrounds. Choose Just chatting, Music & creativity, or Story time for your practice.

A CROWD THAT KEEPS YOU GOING
Locally generated viewers, encouraging comments, and simulated hearts bring your room to life. Add your own comments, pause for a breath, and resume when you are ready.

A LITTLE EXTRA MAGIC
Enjoy random virtual gifts or trigger a Spark, Love note, Bloom, Moon gem, Comet, or Spotlight gift yourself. All gifts are included, have no cash value, and cannot be bought, sold, or redeemed.

CAMERA ON YOUR TERMS
Use an optional camera preview or practice with a studio background. Switch between available front and rear cameras. LiveMuse does not record video or audio and never broadcasts your session.

KEEP YOUR PRACTICE JOURNAL
Finish a session to see your practice time, peak virtual viewers, simulated hearts, and virtual gifts. Your 50 most recent session summaries stay on your device. Delete your history whenever you want.

BUILT FOR YOUR SPACE
Enjoy a dark interface with layouts that adapt to phones, tablets, and larger Android screens.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads. No analytics. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No internet permission. Camera access is optional and used only for local preview.

PLEASE NOTE
LiveMuse is a simulation and entertainment app, not a real streaming service. All audience members, comments generated by the app, likes, and gifts are fictional. It does not connect to social networks, generate real followers, or earn money. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with TikTok or any other social media platform.
