Privacy, by design.

Your practice stays on your device. Here is how LiveMuse handles information.

Effective date: October 21, 2026
Application: LiveMuse — Live Simulator
Package: com.deploydulupulangnanti.fakelivestream
Developer: PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti

LiveMuse has no internet permission, accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or in-app purchases. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app.

Camera access

If you grant camera permission, it is used only for an on-screen preview while your practice room is open. Video is not recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. Audio is not captured; the app does not request microphone permission. You may deny or revoke camera permission in Android Settings and continue using studio backgrounds.

Information stored on your device

If you choose a photo, Android's system picker grants access only to the image you select. LiveMuse stores a resized copy without the original metadata in private app storage for your live background and profile. Photos are never uploaded or shared. Change photo replaces this copy, and Remove photo deletes it. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes it. The app does not request broad access to your photo library.

Your creator name, room preferences, and up to 50 session summaries are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Session summaries contain the room title, session date, duration, and simulated audience metrics. Comments are held only for the current session. Android app backup is disabled.

You can delete session summaries using About → Delete session history. To remove all saved preferences and summaries, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Simulated interactions

Viewers, generated comments, likes, and gifts are fictional and generated on your device. Gifts have no cash value. Nothing is broadcast to other people. LiveMuse does not connect to social networks.

Support and external services

If you select Contact support, your email application handles your message under its own privacy policy. We receive the email address and any information you choose to send and use them only to respond to support requests. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us.

Google Play may process store or purchase-related information under Google's own policies. LiveMuse does not receive payment card details. This privacy-policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process standard request information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This page uses no cookies, analytics, external fonts, or advertising scripts.

Children

LiveMuse is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the app.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date will identify the latest version.
